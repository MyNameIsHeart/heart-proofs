## Morning to noon

- [Barbican Library](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Barbican%20Library%2C%20London) - free, great atmosphere, beautiful architecture outside. Very few toilets in the whole building, though, so expect a line.
- [British Library](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=British%20Library%2C%20London) - free, great atmosphere, silent. Has a gallery of its own.
- [Nagare Bankside](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nagare%20Bankside%2C%20London) - a coffee shop, so a little noisy, but great vibes.
- [Tate Modern Restaurant & Bar, Level 6](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tate%20Modern%2C%20London) - a little noisy, fantastic view. Bar open 10:00-17:30 Mon-Thu, 10:00-22:00 Fri-Sat, 10:00-17:00 Sun.
- [Black Sheep Coffee, 81 Southwark Street](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Black%20Sheep%20Coffee%2C%2081%20Southwark%20Street%2C%20London) - small and busy at peak times, but good coffee and cosy in the mornings.

## Afternoon to evening

- [The Hoxton Holborn](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The%20Hoxton%2C%20Holborn%2C%20London) lobby - a hotel lobby with a bar and good seating, open until late.
- [Royal Festival Hall, Southbank Centre, Level 5](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Royal%20Festival%20Hall%2C%20London), either the bar or the function room - open 10:00-23:00, closed on Mondays. Free, quiet, amazing view, large desks.
- [Ronnie Scott's Jazz Club, 47 Frith Street](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ronnie%20Scott%27s%20Jazz%20Club%2C%2047%20Frith%20Street%2C%20London) - costs money, and the saxophonist was amused by me doing math there, but if you can concentrate through the noise it's a real fun experience to study there, until 3 am (1 am on Tuesdays, midnight on Sundays) and with good music.
- [The Sherlock Holmes, 10 Northumberland Street](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The%20Sherlock%20Holmes%2C%2010%20Northumberland%20Street%2C%20London) - open until 23:00 (22:30 on Sundays). Bonus point to anyone ordering a whisky and soda, like Sherlock does in the books: "I think that I shall have a whisky and soda and a cigar after all this cross-questioning" (The Adventure of the Noble Bachelor).

Bonus recommendation: if it isn't raining too much, use the electric bike throughout the city. You get to see the city on the way, and it's more fun than being packed into the tube.
